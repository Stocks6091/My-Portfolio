About Me

Currently, I am a committed Second-Year Actuarial Science student at the University of the Witwatersrand on a mission to become an actuary as quickly and effectively as possible. Presently, my area of interest involves the point where problem-solving, risk, and technology intersect where problems meet their solutions. My own path has been fueled by my passion for problem-solving and logic, which led me rather naturally into my desire to pursue a career in actuarial science, which requires a strong analytical mind and a perspective that is focused on long-term vision and stability. However, I have also recognized that the modern actuarist is more than just a mathematician; they also need to be digital architects. To this end, I have deliberately developed my technical expertise by taking a 12-day web development intensive boot camp course, among other projects that I have undertaken by myself, such as Python, SQL, and data visualization projects related to probability models and financial simulations, among others. I personally think that actuarial science is more than risk evaluation—it is the application of resilience. My own objective is using emerging tech to make financial and insurance systems more available, more efficient, and more resilient, especially in the African setting. It might involve the automation of analytics, the development of risk dashboards, or the application of sustainable economic models, but it will help close the divide between classic actuarial work and innovative thinking. In addition to my academic and code-related endeavors, I enjoy working in a collaborative and challenge-driven setting and am actively seeking opportunities in internships, research, and mentorship from industry leaders who also see the future in this way.
